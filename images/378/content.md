## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**<br>As/When/Because + S + be + p.p. ~ → (Being) + p.p. ~, S + V.<br>(Being은 생략하는 것이 자연스러움)
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① 부사절의 주어가 주절의 주어와 같은지 확인<br>② 부사절이 수동태(be + p.p.)인지 확인<br>③ 접속사 + 주어 + being을 지우고 p.p.만 남김 (완료형은 Having been + p.p.)
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- 수동 관계인데 ~ing(현재분사)를 쓰는 오류
- 시제 관계를 무시하고 완료형(Having been)을 써야 할 자리에 단순형만 쓰는 오류
- 부정문에서 Not의 위치를 분사 뒤에 두는 오류
**4. 대표 예문(기본형 → 전환형)**
- Because it was damaged in the storm, the roof needed repair.<br>→ Damaged in the storm, the roof needed repair.
- Since it had been reviewed twice, the report was finally approved.<br>→ Having been reviewed twice, the report was finally approved.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트(감점 요인)**
- p.p. 대신 ~ing를 쓴 경우 감점
- 완료 시제가 필요한데 단순 수동분사만 쓴 경우 감점
- Not의 위치 오류 (Not이 분사 앞에 와야 함)

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence**
1. As it was written by a famous author, the novel became a bestseller.
2. Because it had been damaged during shipping, the package was returned.
3. As it is not required for this course, the textbook is optional.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. Writing in French, the letter confused the reader.
2. Damage by the flood, the house had to be rebuilt.
3. Not finish on time, the project caused delays.
**C. Write your own**<br>아래 상황을 수동분사구문을 사용해 한 문장으로 영작해보세요.
- 그 다리는 100년 전에 건설되어서, 지금은 보수가 필요하다.
- 그 이메일은 급하게 작성되어서, 오타가 많았다.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. Written by a famous author, the novel became a bestseller.
2. Having been damaged during shipping, the package was returned.
3. Not required for this course, the textbook is optional.
**B.**
1. Written in French, the letter confused the reader. (Writing → Written, 수동 관계)
2. Damaged by the flood, the house had to be rebuilt. (Damage → Damaged, 분사 형태 오류)
3. Not finished on time, the project caused delays. (Not finish → Not finished, 분사 형태 오류)
**C.** (예시 답안)
- Built 100 years ago, the bridge now needs repair.
- Written in a hurry, the email had many typos.
