## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**<br>that절 (주어 + 과거동사) → having + p.p.
- She said that she had lost her key. → She said having lost her key. (단, that절 주어=주절 주어일 때만 성립)
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① that절의 시제가 주절보다 앞서는지 확인한다<br>② that절 주어와 주절 주어가 같은지 확인한다 (다르면 완료동명사 전환 불가, 의미상 주어 필요)<br>③ having + 과거분사(p.p.) 형태로 바꾸고 접속사/주어를 삭제한다
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- 전치사 뒤에 동사원형을 그대로 쓰는 실수 (for lie ✗ → for having lied ✓)
- 시점 구분 없이 단순동명사(-ing)만 쓰는 실수 (deny breaking ≒ 가능하지만 이전 시점을 명확히 하려면 having broken이 더 정확)
- 완료동명사를 완료부정사(to have p.p.)와 혼동하는 실수
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**
- I regret that I didn't study harder. → I regret not having studied harder.
- They suspected that he had cheated. → They suspected him of having cheated.
- We appreciate that you waited for us. → We appreciate your having waited for us.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**
- having 뒤에 원형이 아닌 p.p.(과거분사)를 쓰지 않은 경우
- 부정문에서 not의 위치를 having 앞이 아닌 뒤에 쓴 경우
- 의미상 주어(소유격/목적격)를 빠뜨린 경우 (주어 불일치 상황)

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence**
1. He admitted that he had cheated on the test.
2. She is ashamed that she lied to her friend.
3. They deny that they damaged the wall.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. I'm sorry for hurt your feelings yesterday.
2. She regrets to waste so much time last year.
3. He denied stole the money from the drawer.
**C. Write your own**
- 완료동명사를 사용해서 "과거에 실수한 것을 후회한다"는 의미의 문장을 하나 만들어 보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. He admitted having cheated on the test.
2. She is ashamed of having lied to her friend.
3. They deny having damaged the wall.
**B.**
1. I'm sorry for having hurt your feelings yesterday.
2. She regrets having wasted so much time last year.
3. He denied having stolen the money from the drawer.
**C.** (예시) I regret not having apologized to her sooner.
