## 📖 Confusion Note

**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**

- assure: (사람에게) 확신·안심을 주다
- ensure: (사물·상황을) 확실하게 보장하다
- insure: (재산·생명 등을) 보험에 들다

**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**
→ **목적어가 무엇인가**를 보면 끝난다.

- 목적어가 **사람** → assure
- 목적어가 **사물/상황(that절)** → ensure
- 목적어가 **보험 대상(재산·생명·건강 등)** → insure

**3. 대표 예문 비교**

- I **assure** you that the flight will not be delayed. (당신에게 확신을 줌)
- We must **ensure** that the flight departs on time. (상황을 보장함)
- Passengers can **insure** their luggage before the flight. (수하물에 보험을 듦)

**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**

- X: We will *ensure* you that the product is safe. → O: We will **assure** you that the product is safe. (사람 목적어에는 ensure 불가)
- X: Please *assure* the door is locked. → O: Please **ensure** the door is locked. (상황 확인에는 assure 불가)
- X: He wants to *assure* his car. → O: He wants to **insure** his car. (보험은 insure만 가능)

**5. 시험 포인트**

- 수능·내신 어법 문제에서 목적어의 종류(사람 vs 사물 vs 보험 대상)를 바꿔치기해서 오답을 유도하는 유형이 자주 출제됨.
- that절 뒤에 사람이 나오면 어색 → assure 의심, 사물/추상명사가 나오면 ensure 의심.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct word**

1. The company tried to (assure / ensure) its customers that the new policy would benefit them.
2. It is the teacher's job to (assure / ensure) that every student understands the rule.
3. Many travelers choose to (ensure / insure) their expensive electronics before a trip.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. The doctor assured that the treatment was effective. → 고치기
2. She wants to ensure her jewelry against loss. → 고치기
3. The airline insured passengers that the flight was safe. → 고치기

**C. Make your own sentence (assure와 ensure 각각 활용)**

1. assure를 사용한 문장 만들기
2. ensure를 사용한 문장 만들기

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. assure (customers = 사람 목적어)
2. ensure (that절 = 상황/결과 보장)
3. insure (전자기기 = 보험 대상)

**B.**

1. The doctor **assured patients** that the treatment was effective. (assure는 반드시 사람 목적어 필요, that절만 단독으로 못 옴)
2. She wants to **insure** her jewelry against loss. (보험 문맥이므로 insure)
3. The airline **assured** passengers that the flight was safe. (승객=사람이므로 insure가 아니라 assure)

**C.** (예시 답안)

1. The coach assured the team that they would win the next game.
2. We need to ensure that the contract terms are clearly stated.
