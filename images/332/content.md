## 📖 Confusion Note
**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**
- despite: ~에도 불구하고 (전치사, preposition)
- although: 비록 ~이지만 (접속사, conjunction)
**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**<br>→ 뒤에 오는 것이 **명사(구)/동명사**면 despite, **주어+동사(완전한 절)**면 although.
**3. 대표 예문 비교**
- Despite the traffic, she arrived early.
- Although there was a lot of traffic, she arrived early.
**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**
- ❌ Despite that he was tired, he kept working.<br>→ ⭕ Although he was tired, he kept working.
- ❌ Despite of the rain, we played outside.<br>→ ⭕ Despite the rain, we played outside. (of 불필요)
- ❌ Although the rain, we played outside.<br>→ ⭕ Although it rained, we played outside. (주어+동사 필요)
**5. 시험 포인트**<br>서술형에서는 "despite of"처럼 of를 붙이는 실수와, although 뒤에 명사만 덜렁 쓰는 실수가 가장 자주 감점 포인트로 출제된다.

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the correct word**
1. ( Despite / Although ) her busy schedule, she always finds time to exercise.
2. ( Despite / Although ) she was exhausted, she finished the marathon.
3. ( Despite / Although ) the small size of the room, it felt cozy.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. Despite of his effort, the team lost the match.
2. Although his effort, the team lost the match.
3. Despite that it was cold, they went hiking.
**C. Make your own sentence (두 단어 각각 활용)**
1. despite를 사용한 문장 만들기
2. although를 사용한 문장 만들기

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. Despite (뒤에 명사구 "her busy schedule")
2. Although (뒤에 주어+동사 "she was exhausted")
3. Despite (뒤에 명사구 "the small size of the room")
**B.**
1. → Despite his effort, the team lost the match. (of 삭제)
2. → Although he made an effort, the team lost the match. (주어+동사로 수정)
3. → Despite the cold, they went hiking. / Although it was cold, they went hiking.
**C.** (예시 답안, 학생 자유 작문 채점)
- Despite the noise, I fell asleep quickly.
- Although I studied hard, I didn't do well on the test.
