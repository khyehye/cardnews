## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**<br>[동사원형 + ing] + [단수동사(is/was)] + ...<br>예) He plays soccer. → Playing soccer is fun.
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① 문장에서 핵심 동사를 찾는다 (plays, skips, exercises 등)<br>② 동사원형에 -ing를 붙여 문장 맨 앞으로 이동시킨다<br>③ 원래 주어(He, She 등)는 삭제하고, 동사는 단수형(is/was)으로 바꾼다<br>④ 부정문은 Ving 앞에 not을 붙인다 (Not eating, Not exercising)
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- 동사원형을 그대로 두고 앞에 놓는 실수 (Play soccer is fun. → X)
- 동명사 주어인데 동사를 복수로 받는 실수 (Playing soccer are fun. → X)
- 부정형을 만들 때 not의 위치를 문장 뒤에 놓는 실수
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**
- They travel a lot. → Traveling a lot is exciting.
- I save money every month. → Saving money every month is important.
- We don't waste time. → Not wasting time is essential.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트**
- Ving 형태가 정확한지 (동사원형+ing 철자 확인, 예: swim → swimming, run → running)
- 동사가 단수(is/was)로 정확히 일치하는지
- 부정문에서 not의 위치가 Ving 바로 앞인지

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence (기본형 → 동명사 주어 문장으로 바꾸세요)**
1. She jogs every morning. → ___
2. He wastes his free time. → ___
3. They don't recycle. → ___
**B. Fix the mistake (틀린 부분을 고치세요)**
1. Swim in the sea are dangerous.
2. Not to study for the exam were a bad idea.
3. Cook dinner is my job every day.
**C. Write your own**<br>아래 동사를 활용해서 동명사 주어 문장을 스스로 만들어보세요.
1. (read) → ___
2. (not / lie) → ___

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. Jogging every morning is her routine. (또는 자유롭게 뒤 내용 구성 가능)
2. Wasting his free time is a bad habit.
3. Not recycling is bad for the environment.
**B.**
1. Swim in the sea are dangerous. → **Swimming** in the sea **is** dangerous.
2. Not to study for the exam were a bad idea. → **Not studying** for the exam **was** a bad idea.
3. Cook dinner is my job every day. → **Cooking** dinner is my job every day.
**C.** (예시 답안)
1. Reading books is fun.
2. Not lying to your friends is important.
