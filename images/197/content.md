## 📖 Confusion Note

**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**

- economic: 경제(economy)와 직접 관련된, 객관적·전문적인 뜻의 형용사
- economical: 돈·시간·자원을 절약하는, 실속 있다는 뜻의 형용사

**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**
뒤에 오는 명사가 policy, crisis, growth, system처럼 "경제 그 자체"를 가리키면 economic. money, time, fuel, car처럼 "아껴 쓰는 대상"이 함께 오면 economical.

**3. 대표 예문 비교**

- economic: The economic recovery took three years. (경제 회복 그 자체)
- economical: Buying in bulk is more economical. (대량 구매가 더 절약이 된다)

**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**

- X: an economic way to save money → O: an economical way to save money
- X: economical growth of the country → O: economic growth of the country

**5. 시험 포인트**
빈칸 앞뒤 명사를 먼저 확인하는 습관이 핵심. "정책/위기/성장" 계열 명사 → economic, "절약/효율/비용" 계열 명사 → economical.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct word**

1. The country is going through a serious (economic / economical) downturn.
2. It's more (economic / economical) to cook at home than eat out every day.
3. The professor gave a lecture on (economic / economical) theory.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. This economical crisis has lasted for two years.
2. She bought an economic washing machine that uses less water.
3. The government's economical policy was announced yesterday.

**C. Make your own sentence (두 단어 각각 활용)**

- economic을 사용한 문장 1개
- economical을 사용한 문장 1개

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.** 1. economic  2. economical  3. economic

**B.**

1. This economic crisis has lasted for two years. (경제 위기 자체 → economic)
2. She bought an economical washing machine that uses less water. (절약형 → economical)
3. The government's economic policy was announced yesterday. (경제 정책 → economic)

**C.** 예시 답안

- economic: The two countries signed a new economic agreement.
- economical: Taking the bus is a more economical choice than driving.
