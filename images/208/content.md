## 📖 Confusion Note

**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**

- speak: 언어를 구사하거나 격식 있게 혹은 일방적으로 말하는 행위
- talk: 누군가와 편하게 대화를 주고받는 행위

**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**
"상대와 상호작용하며 대화를 나누는가?" → Yes면 talk, No(언어 능력·일방적 발화)면 speak

**3. 대표 예문 비교**

- She can speak Spanish. (언어 구사 능력)
- We talked about the trip. (상호 대화)
- He spoke to the class about safety. (일방적 발화, 격식)

**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X→O)**

- X: I want to talk English well. → O: I want to speak English well. (언어 구사 능력은 speak)
- X: He speaks with his friends every day. → O: He talks with his friends every day. (친구와의 편한 대화는 talk)

**5. 시험 포인트**
뒤에 언어명(영어, 프랑스어 등)이 바로 오면 speak, "with/to + 사람"과 함께 자연스러운 대화 상황이면 talk가 자주 출제돼요.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct word**

1. My little brother is learning to (speak / talk) his first words.
2. Can we (speak / talk) about the party plans later?
3. The president will (speak / talk) to the nation tonight.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. She can talk three languages fluently.
2. I need to speak with you about something important, can you talk for a minute?
3. He speaks with his classmates during lunch break.

**C. Make your own sentence**
speak와 talk를 각각 활용해서 나만의 문장을 하나씩 만들어보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. speak (언어 구사 능력)
2. talk (상호 대화)
3. speak (공식 발화/연설)

**B.**

1. She can speak three languages fluently. (언어 능력 → speak)
2. I need to talk with you about something important — do you have a minute? (편한 대화 → talk, 문장도 자연스럽게 수정)
3. He talks with his classmates during lunch break. (친구와의 편한 대화 → talk)

**C.** (예시 답안)

- I'm trying to speak more confidently in English class.
- We talked for hours about our favorite movies.
