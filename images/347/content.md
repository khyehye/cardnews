## 📖 Confusion Note
**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**
- because: 접속사. '~때문에'라는 이유의 절을 이끈다.
- because of: 전치사구. '~때문에'라는 이유의 명사(구)를 이끈다.
**2. 구별 포인트**<br>뒤에 오는 것이 '주어+동사'를 갖춘 완전한 절이면 because, 명사나 명사구 하나면 because of. 판단 기준은 딱 하나: 뒤에 동사가 있는가.
**3. 대표 예문 비교**
- She stayed home because she was sick. (because + 절: she was sick)
- She stayed home because of her illness. (because of + 명사: her illness)
**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**
- X: I was tired because of I slept late. → O: I was tired because I slept late.
- X: I was tired because lack of sleep. → O: I was tired because of lack of sleep.
- X: because of it was raining → O: because it was raining
**5. 시험 포인트**<br>객관식에서는 빈칸 뒤 형태(절 vs 명사구)를 먼저 확인하게 하는 문제가 자주 나온다. 서술형에서는 because of 뒤에 동명사(-ing)나 명사절을 잘못 붙이는 실수가 감점 포인트다.

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the correct word**
1. The flight was delayed ___ (because / because of) technical problems.
2. He couldn't focus ___ (because / because of) he was so tired.
3. The picnic was postponed ___ (because / because of) the rain.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. She was upset because of she failed the test.
2. We left early because of it got dark.
3. He passed the exam because his hard work.
**C. Make your own sentence**
- because를 사용한 문장 1개
- because of를 사용한 문장 1개

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. because of (technical problems = 명사구)
2. because (he was so tired = 절)
3. because of (the rain = 명사)
**B.**
1. She was upset because she failed the test. (because of 뒤에 절이 와서 틀림)
2. We left early because it got dark. (because of 뒤에 절이 와서 틀림)
3. He passed the exam because of his hard work. (because 뒤에 명사구가 와서 틀림)
**C.** (자유 작문 — 학생 답안에 따라 채점)
