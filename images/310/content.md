## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**
- will + 동사원형 (조동사이므로 형태 변화 없음)
- be동사(am/is/are) + going to + 동사원형
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① 원래 문장에서 본동사를 찾는다<br>② 주어를 확인한다 (be동사 선택에 영향)<br>③ will을 붙이거나, be동사 + going to + 동사원형으로 바꾼다
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- will 뒤에 3인칭 단수 -s를 붙이는 실수 (goes, does 등)
- be going to에서 주어에 맞지 않는 be동사 사용 (I is going to 등)
- 부정문에서 not의 위치를 잘못 넣는 경우 (will not, be동사 + not + going to)
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형→전환형)**
- He plays soccer. → He will play soccer. / He is going to play soccer.
- We eat lunch at noon. → We will eat lunch at noon. / We are going to eat lunch at noon.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**
- 동사원형을 지키지 않으면 감점
- 주어-be동사 일치 오류는 감점
- will과 be going to를 섞어 쓰는 경우 (예: will going to) 감점

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence (will 또는 be going to 사용)**
1. I finish my homework. (will)
2. She washes the dishes. (be going to)
3. They travel to Busan. (will)
4. He watches TV after dinner. (be going to)
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. I will goes to school early tomorrow.
2. She is going to plays the piano tonight.
3. We will visits our uncle next week.
**C. Write your own**
- will을 사용해서 내일 할 일에 대한 문장을 하나 써보세요.
- be going to를 사용해서 이번 주말 계획에 대한 문장을 하나 써보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. I will finish my homework.
2. She is going to wash the dishes.
3. They will travel to Busan.
4. He is going to watch TV after dinner.
**B.**
1. I will go to school early tomorrow. (goes → go, will 뒤엔 동사원형)
2. She is going to play the piano tonight. (plays → play, going to 뒤엔 동사원형)
3. We will visit our uncle next week. (visits → visit, will 뒤엔 동사원형)
**C.** (자유 작성 — 예시: I will finish my project tonight. / We are going to go camping this weekend.)
