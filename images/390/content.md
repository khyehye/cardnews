## 📖 Expression Note
**1. 쓰는 상황**<br>자신의 의견을 사실처럼 단정하지 않고, 근거가 있는 주장처럼 부드럽게 제시하고 싶을 때 사용한다. 상대의 반박 여지를 열어두면서도 설득력을 유지하는 완곡어법(hedging expression)이다.
**2. 격식도**<br>격식 있는 자리 전용 표현이다. 캐주얼한 대화에서는 "I think~", "I feel like~" 정도면 충분하지만, 이메일·보고서·프레젠테이션·회의처럼 공적인 자리에서는 "It could be argued that~"류 표현이 훨씬 자연스럽고 전문적으로 들린다.
**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**
- (이메일) It could be argued that the delay was unavoidable given the circumstances.
- (회의) One could argue that this approach saves more time in the long run.
- (프레젠테이션) It could be said that our strategy paid off this quarter.
**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**
- One could argue that~ (누군가는 ~라고 주장할 수도 있다)
- It could be said that~ (~라고 말할 수 있다)
- There is a case to be made that~ (~라는 근거가 있다)
- Some might contend that~ (더 격식 있는 학술적 뉘앙스)
**5. 원어민 variation**<br>학술 논문·리포트에서는 "It has been suggested that~", "Arguably, ~" 같은 변형도 자주 쓰인다. "Arguably"는 문장 맨 앞에 붙여 훨씬 간결하게 같은 뉘앙스를 낼 수 있다 (예: Arguably, remote work increases productivity.).

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the natural expression**
1. (Formal report) _____ the merger will benefit both companies long-term.<br>a) I think b) It could be argued that c) Whatever d) I guess
2. (Casual chat with a friend) _____ this restaurant has the best pasta in town.<br>a) It could be argued that b) One could contend that c) I think d) There is a case to be made that
3. (Academic essay) _____ social media has changed how we communicate.<br>a) I feel b) It could be argued that c) Kinda d) Sorta
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. It could be argue that the plan needs more funding. → (동사 형태 오류 수정)
2. One could to argue that prices are too high. → (불필요한 to 삭제)
3. There is a case to made for shorter meetings. → (be 누락 수정)
**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**<br>아래 상황에 맞춰 "It could be argued that~" 또는 유의 표현을 활용해 문장을 만들어보세요.
1. 회사의 재택근무 정책에 대한 의견을 이메일로 전달하는 상황
2. 팀 회의에서 새로운 마케팅 전략을 제안하는 상황

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.** 1. b) It could be argued that  2. c) I think  3. b) It could be argued that
**B.**
1. It could be argue → It could **be argued** (수동태 조동사 뒤에는 과거분사)
2. One could to argue → One could **argue** (조동사 could 뒤에는 원형, to 불필요)
3. to made → to **be made** (There is a case to be made 구문 고정 표현)
**C.** (예시 답안)
1. It could be argued that a flexible remote-work policy would improve employee retention.
2. One could argue that a digital-first marketing strategy would reach more younger customers.
