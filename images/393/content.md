## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**<br>As/Because + S + had p.p. ~, S + V ... → Having p.p. ~, S + V ...<br>(수동일 경우: As + S + had been p.p. ~ → Having been p.p. ~)
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① 두 절 중 시간상 먼저 일어난 절을 찾는다 (had p.p.가 있는 절)<br>② 접속사와 반복되는 주어를 지운다<br>③ had p.p. → Having p.p.로 바꾼다 (수동이면 Having been p.p., 부정이면 Not having p.p.)
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- 시간차가 없는데 무조건 Having을 쓰는 실수 (동시동작이면 단순 -ing)
- 수동태인데 having p.p.만 쓰고 been을 빠뜨리는 실수
- 부정어 not의 위치를 having 뒤에 두는 실수 (having not X → not having O)
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**
- As she had lost her passport, she couldn't board the plane.<br>→ Having lost her passport, she couldn't board the plane.
- Because he had been warned twice, he took the rule seriously.<br>→ Having been warned twice, he took the rule seriously.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**
- Having 뒤 동사를 원형으로 쓴 경우 (p.p.가 아닌 동사원형 사용)
- 수동 표현에서 been 누락
- 시간차가 없는 문장에 억지로 완료분사구문을 적용해 의미가 어색해진 경우

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence**
1. As he had finished all his chores, he went out to play.
2. Because she had been chosen as the leader, she felt responsible.
3. As they had not prepared for the trip, they missed the flight.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. Having finish his homework, he watched TV. (오류 수정)
2. Having invited to the party, she was very happy. (오류 수정)
3. Having not eaten breakfast, he felt dizzy. (오류 수정)
**C. Write your own**
- 완료분사구문(Having p.p.)을 사용해서, 어제 있었던 일을 시간 순서대로 표현하는 문장을 만들어보세요. (예: 숙제를 끝낸 뒤 게임을 한 상황 등 자유 주제)

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. Having finished all his chores, he went out to play.
2. Having been chosen as the leader, she felt responsible.
3. Not having prepared for the trip, they missed the flight.
**B.**
1. Having finished his homework, he watched TV. (finish → finished, p.p. 형태로 수정)
2. Having been invited to the party, she was very happy. (수동이므로 been 추가)
3. Not having eaten breakfast, he felt dizzy. (not의 위치를 having 앞으로 이동)
**C.**<br>(자유 작문 — Having p.p. 형태와 시간차 표현이 올바른지, 수동/부정 처리가 정확한지 확인)
