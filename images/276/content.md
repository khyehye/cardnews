## 📖 Confusion Note
**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**
- continual: 반복되지만 중간에 멈춤(gap)이 있는 상태. "Repeated with breaks."
- continuous: 처음부터 끝까지 끊기지 않고 이어지는 상태. "Uninterrupted."
**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**<br>"중간에 멈췄다가 다시 시작하는가?" → 그렇다면 continual, 한 번도 멈추지 않는다면 continuous. (Interruption test)
**3. 대표 예문 비교**
- The continual power outages disrupted the factory's schedule. (정전이 반복적으로 일어남, 중간에 복구됐다가 다시 끊김)
- The continuous hum of the air conditioner never stopped all night. (에어컨 소음이 밤새 한 번도 안 멈춤)
**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**
- X: The continuous interruptions during the debate were annoying. (방해는 반복되는 것이지 끊김없는 것이 아님)
- O: The continual interruptions during the debate were annoying.
- X: We need a continual internet connection for the live broadcast. (생방송은 끊기면 안 되므로 continuous가 맞음)
- O: We need a continuous internet connection for the live broadcast.
**5. 시험 포인트**<br>수능/공무원 어휘 문제에서는 문맥에 '반복성(재발)'을 나타내는 단서(again, every few minutes, repeatedly 등)가 있으면 continual, '중단 없음'을 나타내는 단서(without stopping, all night, nonstop 등)가 있으면 continuous를 선택하게 만든다.

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the correct word**
1. The (continual / continuous) beeping of the smoke detector stopped every few minutes before starting again.
2. Employees complained about the (continual / continuous) noise from the highway that never seemed to pause.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. The continual flow of the river never paused, even during the drought.
2. Her continuous absences — she missed class every other week — worried her teacher.
**C. Make your own sentence (두 단어 각각 활용)**
- Write one sentence using "continual" to describe something that happens repeatedly with gaps.
- Write one sentence using "continuous" to describe something that never stops.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. continual (beeping stops and starts — repeated with gaps)
2. continuous (noise never pauses — uninterrupted)
**B.**
1. X → O: continuous flow (a river's flow doesn't stop and restart, it's uninterrupted)
2. X → O: continual absences (missing class "every other week" means repeated with gaps, not nonstop)
**C.** (모범답안 예시)
- continual: The continual traffic jams on this road make my commute unpredictable.
- continuous: The factory runs on a continuous production line 24 hours a day.
