## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**<br>as + 형용사/부사 원급 + as + 비교대상(possible / ever / can be 등)
**2. 단계별 순서**<br>① 기본 문장에서 강조하고 싶은 형용사/부사를 찾는다<br>② as ~ as 틀에 원급 그대로 넣는다 (비교급 X)<br>③ 관용 확장어(possible, ever, can be)나 비교대상을 붙여 의미를 완성한다
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- as ~ as 사이에 비교급(-er/more)을 쓰는 실수
- possible 자리에 다른 형용사를 넣어 의미가 어색해지는 경우
- not so much A as B에서 A/B 순서를 뒤바꾸는 실수
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**
- He is very kind. → He is as kind as can be.
- Finish it quickly. → Finish it as quickly as possible.
- She is still beautiful. → She is as beautiful as ever.
- He is lazy, but more cautious. → He is not so much lazy as cautious.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**
- as ~ as 사이 원급 위반 시 감점
- possible/ever 등 관용 확장어 누락 시 의미 미완성으로 감점
- not so much A as B 어순 오류 시 감점

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence**
1. She is very honest. (as ~ as can be 사용)
2. Reply quickly. (as ~ as possible 사용)
3. He still runs fast. (as ~ as ever 사용)
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. He is as more diligent as possible.
2. She is as beautifuler as ever.
3. He is not so lazy much as careless.
**C. Write your own**
- as ~ as possible을 사용해 나만의 문장 1개 만들기
- not so much A as B를 사용해 나만의 문장 1개 만들기

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. She is as honest as can be.
2. Reply as quickly as possible.
3. He still runs as fast as ever.
**B.**
1. He is as diligent as possible. (비교급 more 삭제, 원급 유지)
2. She is as beautiful as ever. (비교급 -er 삭제)
3. He is not so much lazy as careless. (어순 수정: not so much A as B)
**C.** (자유 작문 — 형식만 확인)
