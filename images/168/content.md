## 📖 Writing Pattern Note

**1. 전환 공식**

- no matter who → whoever
- no matter what → whatever
- no matter which → whichever
- no matter when → whenever
- no matter where → wherever
- no matter how (+형용사/부사) → however (+형용사/부사)

**2. 단계별 순서**
① no matter + 의문사 구문 확인
② 의문사를 복합관계사로 교체 (however는 형용사/부사를 앞에 붙임)
③ 콤마(,)로 주절 연결

**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**

- however 뒤에 형용사/부사를 빠뜨리고 바로 주어+동사를 쓰는 오류
- whoever/whatever를 명사절 자리(주어/목적어)에 쓰는 것과 부사절(양보) 자리에 쓰는 것을 혼동
- whichever와 whatever의 선택 범위(한정 vs 비한정) 혼동

**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**

- No matter who you meet → Whoever you meet, be polite.
- No matter what happens → Whatever happens, don't panic.
- No matter how busy you are → However busy you are, call me back.

**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**

- however 뒤 형용사/부사 누락
- 콤마 누락 또는 잘못된 위치
- 복합관계사 뒤 어순(의문문 어순 잔존) 오류

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Transform the sentence (복합관계사로 바꿔 쓰기)**

1. No matter what you choose, I'll respect it.
2. No matter how strange the story sounds, it's true.
3. No matter which team wins, the fans will celebrate.

**B. Fix the mistake (어순 오류 찾아 고치기)**

1. However you are busy, please reply soon.
2. Whatever happens it, we'll face it together.
3. Whichever choose you, the result is the same.

**C. Write your own**

- "아무리 ~해도" (however)를 활용해 한 문장 작문
- "누가 ~하든" (whoever)을 활용해 한 문장 작문

---

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. Whatever you choose, I'll respect it.
2. However strange the story sounds, it's true.
3. Whichever team wins, the fans will celebrate.

**B.**

1. However busy you are, please reply soon. → however 뒤엔 형용사(busy)가 바로 와야 함
2. Whatever happens, we'll face it together. → happens 뒤 목적어 it 불필요(자동사)
3. Whichever you choose, the result is the same. → 의문문 어순(choose you) 아닌 평서문 어순(you choose)

**C.** (예시 답안)

- However tired I am, I always finish my homework.
- Whoever calls, I won't pick up during the exam.
