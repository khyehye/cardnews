## 📖 Writing Pattern Note

**1. 전환 공식**

- say "Verb~" → tell/ask + 목적어 + to + 동사원형
- say "Don't Verb~" → tell/ask + 목적어 + not to + 동사원형

**2. 단계별 순서**
① 인용동사 결정 (지시 → tell / 부탁 → ask)
② 목적어(듣는 사람) 삽입
③ (not) + to + 동사원형 순서로 마무리 — 시제·인칭대명사 문맥에 맞게 조정

**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**

- to 누락: told me not be late (X)
- 순서 오류: told me don't to be late (X)
- tell/ask 뒤 목적어 생략
- 시제·인칭대명사(said → told, this → that 등) 조정 누락

**4. 대표 예문(기본형→전환형)**

- "Turn off the light." → He told me to turn off the light.
- "Don't run in the hallway." → The teacher told us not to run in the hallway.
- "Please send me the file." → She asked me to send her the file.

**5. 서술형 채점 포인트(감점 요인)**

- to부정사 형태 오류 (동사원형 대신 동사 변형 사용)
- not의 위치 오류 (to 뒤에 놓는 실수)
- 인칭대명사·소유격 미조정 (me/her 등 문맥 반영 안 함)
- 인용동사 선택 오류 (지시 상황에 ask 사용 등)

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct transformation**

1. She said, "Wait here." → She told me ___.
① wait here ② to wait here ③ waiting here ④ don't wait here
2. He said, "Don't be late." → He told them ___.
① not to be late ② to not be late ③ don't be late ④ not being late

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. He told me don't to touch the stove. →
2. She asked to me clean my room. →
3. The coach told us not be nervous. →

**C. Make your own sentence**

1. 직접화법 명령문(긍정) 하나를 만들고, tell을 이용해 간접화법으로 전환하세요.
2. 직접화법 명령문(부정) 하나를 만들고, ask를 이용해 간접화법으로 전환하세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.** 1-② to wait here / 2-① not to be late

**B.**

1. He told me not to touch the stove. (not+to 순서 수정)
2. She asked me to clean my room. (asked 뒤 목적어 형태 me로 수정, to 추가)
3. The coach told us not to be nervous. (to 누락 수정)

**C.** (예시)

- He said, "Turn in your homework." → He told us to turn in our homework.
- She said, "Don't open the box." → She asked me not to open the box.
