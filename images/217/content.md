## 📖 Expression Note

**1. 쓰는 상황**
옷가게, 신발가게, 서점, 온라인 쇼핑몰 등에서 직원이 "도와드릴까요?"라고 물을 때, 아직 구매 의사가 없거나 혼자 둘러보고 싶을 때 사용합니다.

**2. 격식도**
캐주얼한 일상 표현입니다. 무례하지 않으면서도 편하게 쓸 수 있는 톤이라 점원-손님 사이 짧은 대화에 자주 등장합니다.

**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**

- Clerk: Hi, is there anything I can help you with?
- Me: I'm just looking, thanks!

**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**

- Just browsing, thanks.
- I'm good for now, thank you.
- I'll shout if I need anything!

**5. 원어민 variation**

- 아주 캐주얼한 사이: "Nah, just poking around!"
- 조금 더 정중한 매장: "I'm just having a look, but thank you for asking."

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the natural expression**

1. A clerk offers help but you just want to browse alone. What's the best response?
(a) I'm good for now, thanks! (b) You must help me right now. (c) I already bought everything.
2. Which sounds most natural when ending the conversation politely?
(a) I will terminate this interaction. (b) I'll shout if I need anything! (c) Stop asking me.

**B. Complete the dialogue**

1. Clerk: Let me know if you need anything. / Me: Sure, ___ ___ if I do! (I will shout / shout I will / will I shout)
2. Agent: Would you like recommendations? / Me: Not right now, I'm ___ for now. (good / goods / well)

**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**
아래 상황에 맞춰 나만의 대화를 만들어보세요: 문구점에서 점원이 "필요한 거 있으세요?"라고 묻습니다. 자연스럽게 거절하고 계속 구경하겠다는 대화를 완성해보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.** 1. (a) I'm good for now, thanks! 2. (b) I'll shout if I need anything!

**B.** 1. I will shout 2. good

**C.** 예시 답안: Clerk: Anything I can help with? / Me: I'm just looking around, thanks — I'll shout if I need anything!
