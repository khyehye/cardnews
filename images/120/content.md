## 📖 Expression Note

**1. 쓰는 상황**
회사 이메일, 공지문, 안내문, 고객 응대 메일 등 격식이 필요한 서면 커뮤니케이션에서 소식을 전달할 때 쓴다. 구어체 대화에서는 거의 쓰지 않는다.

**2. 격식도 (캐주얼 ↔ 포멀)**

- 캐주얼: Just so you know, / Heads up,
- 중간: I wanted to let you know that~
- 포멀(오늘의 표현): I am writing to inform you that~ / Please be advised that~ / We regret to inform you that~

**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**

- (메일 도입부) *I am writing to inform you that our office will be relocating next month.*
- (안내문) *Please be advised that the annual maintenance will take place this Saturday.*
- (거절 메일) *We regret to inform you that we are unable to proceed with your request at this time.*

**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**
This is to inform you that~ / Kindly be informed that~ / We wish to inform you that~ / I would like to inform you that~

**5. 원어민 variation**
격식도를 한 단계 낮추고 싶을 때는 "I wanted to let you know that~" 또는 "Just a quick note to say~"로 바꿔 쓴다. 반대로 더 딱딱한 공문서 톤이 필요하면 "This is to inform you that~"을 선호한다.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the natural expression**

1. (공지) "_____ the parking lot will be closed for repairs next week."
① I'm just saying ② Please be advised that ③ You should know
2. (안 좋은 소식) "_____ your reservation could not be confirmed."
① We regret to inform you that ② We're happy to say ③ FYI
3. (주의 환기) "I'd like to _____ a discrepancy in the attached report."
① bring to your attention ② tell you about ③ mention casually
4. (공문서 톤) "_____ all employees must complete the training by Friday."
① Hey everyone ② This is to inform you that ③ Just letting you know
5. (좋은 소식, 정중) "_____ your application has been accepted."
① We wish to inform you that ② Guess what ③ FYI

**B. Complete the dialogue**

1. A: Did you send the update to the client?
B: Yes, I ___________ (~라고 알렸어요) the delivery date has changed.
2. A: How should I start the rejection email?
B: Start with "We ___________ (유감스럽게도 알려드립니다) your proposal was not selected."
3. A: The report has a typo, how do I mention it politely?
B: Say "I'd like to ___________ (주의를 환기시키다) a small error on page 3."

**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**

1. 학교 행사 일정 변경을 알리는 공지문을 "Please be advised that~"을 사용해서 1문장 작성하세요.
2. 지원자에게 불합격 소식을 정중하게 전하는 이메일 문장을 "We regret to inform you that~"을 사용해서 작성하세요.
3. 회사 동료에게 회의 시간 변경을 알리는 대화문을 "I am writing to inform you that~" 또는 "I wanted to let you know that~" 중 상황에 맞게 골라 2줄로 작성하세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.** 1. ② 2. ① 3. ① 4. ② 5. ①

**B.**

1. informed you that (예: I informed you that the delivery date has changed.)
2. regret to inform you that
3. bring to your attention (예: I'd like to bring to your attention a small error on page 3.)

**C.** 정해진 정답 없음 — 표현 사용 여부와 격식도가 상황에 맞는지 확인.
