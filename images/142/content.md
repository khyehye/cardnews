## 📖 Expression Note

**1. 쓰는 상황**
식사 직전(배고픔) 또는 식사 직후(배부름)에 자신의 상태를 말할 때 쓰는 일상 표현.

**2. 격식도**
전부 캐주얼(비격식) 표현. 친구, 가족과의 대화, 문자, 톡에 자연스럽게 사용. 격식적인 자리(면접, 발표)에서는 "I don't have much of an appetite" 같은 완곡 표현이 더 어울림.

**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**

- A: Lunch is almost ready! B: Great, I'm starving!
- A: More noodles? B: I'm full, but thank you!
- I only had a small breakfast, so I'm pretty hungry by 11am.
- After Thanksgiving dinner, everyone was completely stuffed.

**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**

- (배고픔) I could eat a horse. / My stomach is growling.
- (배부름) I've had enough. / I can't eat another thing.

**5. 원어민 variation**

- starving은 실제로 굶주린 상태가 아니어도 과장해서 자주 씀 (informal hyperbole).
- stuffed는 명절/파티처럼 과식한 상황에서 특히 자주 등장.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the natural expression**

1. You haven't eaten all day. How do you feel?
① I'm full ② I'm starving ③ I'm stuffed
2. You just finished a huge Thanksgiving dinner.
① I'm hungry ② I'm starving ③ I'm stuffed

**B. Complete the dialogue**

1. A: Do you want some more cake? B: No thanks, I'm ______. (배부름, 기본)
2. A: You look tired. B: Yeah, and I'm really ______ too — I skipped lunch. (배고픔, 강조)

**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**
자신이 아침을 거르고 학교에 온 상황을 가정해, starving을 활용한 짧은 대화(2줄)를 만들어 보세요.

---

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.** 1. ② I'm starving  2. ③ I'm stuffed

**B.** 1. full  2. starving

**C. (예시 답안)**
A: You didn't eat breakfast? B: No, I'm starving — let's grab something quick!
