## 📖 Expression Note

**1. 쓰는 상황**
직접 "I think~"라고 말하면 지나치게 단정적이거나 무례하게 들릴 수 있는 상황 — 상사에게 우려를 전달할 때, 클라이언트 이메일에서 문제를 지적할 때, 회의에서 반대 의견을 낼 때 사용한다.

**2. 격식도**
전부 격식체(formal register)에 속하며, 캐주얼한 대화에서는 다소 딱딱하게 들릴 수 있다. 이메일, 보고서, 공식 회의 발언에 적합하다.

**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**

- Email: "It seems to me that we haven't accounted for shipping delays."
- Meeting: "It appears that the client's expectations have shifted."
- Casual-formal mix: "I get the impression that the team needs more support."

**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**

- In my view / In my opinion (더 직접적)
- It would appear that (더 격식적, 다소 딱딱함)
- I can't help but think that (개인적 뉘앙스 강함)

**5. 원어민 variation**

- "It seems (to me) like~" (구어체에 가까움, seems 뒤에 like도 자주 씀)
- "If I'm reading this correctly, it seems that~" (근거를 먼저 언급하는 패턴)
- 문장 끝에 "..., or so it seems." 로 마무리하는 경우도 있음

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the natural expression**

1. (In my opinion / It appears that) the budget report has an error in section 3.
2. I (get the impression / seems to me) that our client wants a faster turnaround.
3. (From what I can tell / I think so), the vendor hasn't confirmed the delivery date.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. It seems to I that we should delay the announcement.
2. It appear that the new policy isn't working well.
3. I get impression that he's overwhelmed with the project.

**C. Make your own sentence**
아래 상황에 맞춰 완곡 표현을 사용한 문장을 하나씩 작성해보세요.

1. 동료의 업무 방식에 우려가 있을 때
2. 데이터를 근거로 조심스럽게 결론을 내릴 때

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. It appears that
2. get the impression
3. From what I can tell

**B.**

1. It seems to **me** that we should delay the announcement.
2. It **appears** that the new policy isn't working well.
3. I get **the** impression that he's overwhelmed with the project.

**C.** (예시 답안)

1. It seems to me that his approach might slow down the whole team.
2. From what I can tell, the numbers support a more cautious strategy.
