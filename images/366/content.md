## 📖 Confusion Note
**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**
- as: 전치사/접속사로 "~로서, ~대로" — 실제 신분, 자격, 역할, 방식을 나타냄
- like: 전치사로 "~처럼, ~같이" — 실제로는 아니지만 유사함, 비유를 나타냄
**2. 구별 포인트**<br>주어가 뒤에 오는 명사와 **실제로 동일한 존재/행위인가**를 확인한다. 동일하면 as, 겉모습·행동만 비슷하면 like.
**3. 대표 예문 비교**
- As a lawyer, he defends clients in court. (실제 변호사)
- He argues like a lawyer, but he's actually an engineer. (변호사가 아님, 비유)
**4. 자주 하는 실수**
- ✗ He works like a teacher. (실제 교사인데 like 사용 → 의미가 "교사처럼 행동하지만 교사는 아님"으로 바뀜)
- ✓ He works as a teacher.
- ✗ As many people think, cats hate water. (X — 이 구문은 "as many people think"처럼 절과 함께 쓰이는 경우 의미 확인 필요)
- ✓ As many people think, cats dislike water. (as + 절: ~하듯이)
**5. 시험 포인트**
- "실제 신분/자격을 나타내는 전치사구"인지 "겉모습만 비슷한 비유"인지 문맥으로 판단하는 문제가 자주 출제됨
- as ~ as 원급 비교 구문과 혼동하지 않도록 주의 (No one knows her as well as her sister does.)

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the correct word**
1. My father works ______ a firefighter. He's been doing it for 20 years. (as / like)
2. She sang ______ an angel — everyone was amazed. (as / like)
3. ______ the manual says, turn off the power first. (As / Like)
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. He behaves as a child, even though he's 30 years old.
2. Like a member of this team, you must attend every meeting.
**C. Make your own sentence**<br>Write one sentence using "as" to describe a real role, and one sentence using "like" to describe a comparison.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. as (실제 소방관)
2. like (천사는 아니지만 비유)
3. As (as + 절: ~하는 대로)
**B.**
1. He behaves **like** a child, even though he's 30 years old. (실제 어린이가 아니므로 like)
2. **As** a member of this team, you must attend every meeting. (실제 팀원이므로 as)
**C.** (예시)
- As a nurse, she takes care of patients every day.
- The city looks like a painting at sunset.
