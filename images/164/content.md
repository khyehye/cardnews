## 📖 Writing Pattern Note

**1. 전환 공식**

- 의문사 있음: 의문사 + S + V (평서문 어순)
- 의문사 없음(Yes/No): if/whether + S + V

**2. 단계별 순서**
① said → asked로 동사 교체
② 의문사 유무 확인 → 어순 결정
③ do/does/did 삭제하고 본동사에 시제 반영
④ 인칭대명사·시간/장소부사 변화 (I→he, now→then, today→that day 등)

**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**

- 의문사절 안에서 의문문 어순(주어-동사 도치)을 그대로 쓰는 실수 (What did I want ❌)
- if/whether 없이 Yes/No 질문을 평서문처럼 붙여버리는 실수
- do/does/did를 지우지 않고 본동사와 같이 남겨두는 실수

**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**

- "What is your name?" → He asked what my name was.
- "Can you swim?" → She asked if I could swim.

**5. 서술형 채점 포인트(감점 요인)**

- 의문사절 어순 오류 (도치 형태 그대로 사용)
- if/whether 누락
- 시제 불일치(현재형 그대로 사용)
- 인칭대명사 미변환

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Transform the sentence**

1. He said, "What is your name?" → He asked ___.
2. She said, "Can you swim?" → She asked ___.
3. They said, "Why are you late?" → They asked ___.
4. He said, "Did you finish your homework?" → He asked ___.
5. She said, "How much does it cost?" → She asked ___.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. He asked what did she want.
2. She asked if do I like pizza.
3. They asked where was he going.
4. He asked that if I was ready.
5. She asked what time does the movie start.

**C. Write your own**

1. 다음 직접화법 질문을 간접화법으로 바꿔 문장을 만들어보세요: "Where do you work?"
2. 다음 직접화법 질문을 간접화법으로 바꿔 문장을 만들어보세요: "Are you coming to the party?"
3. 자신이 최근에 들었던 질문 하나를 골라 직접화법 → 간접화법으로 직접 전환해보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. He asked what my name was.
2. She asked if I could swim.
3. They asked why I was late.
4. He asked if I had finished my homework.
5. She asked how much it cost.

**B.**

1. He asked what she wanted. (의문사절도 평서문 어순 → 도치 제거)
2. She asked if I liked pizza. (do 삭제, 시제를 동사에 반영)
3. They asked where he was going. (의문사절 어순: where + S + V)
4. He asked if I was ready. (if/whether 앞에 that을 겹쳐 쓰지 않음)
5. She asked what time the movie started. (does 삭제 + 과거시제 반영)

**C.**

- 예시: He asked where I worked. / She asked if I was coming to the party.
- (자유 작문이므로 학생 답안은 위 전환 공식 5단계를 지켰는지로 채점)
