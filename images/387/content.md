## 📖 Exam Trap Note
**1. 함정 포인트**<br>Were it not for(현재 가정)와 Had it not been for(과거 가정)를 혼용하거나, 도치 형태와 결과절 동사 시제를 짝이 안 맞게 쓰는 것이 핵심 함정이다.
**2. 판별 공식/체크리스트**
- 결과절이 `would/could/might + 동사원형` → 앞에는 반드시 **Were it not for**
- 결과절이 `would/could/might have p.p.` → 앞에는 반드시 **Had it not been for**
- 도치 주어 자리에 it이 오는지, 뒤에 for + 명사구가 오는지 확인
**3. 오답 예문 vs 정답 예문**
- 오답: Were it not for the map, we would have gotten lost. (현재형 도치 + 과거완료 결과절 불일치)
- 정답: Had it not been for the map, we would have gotten lost.
- 오답: Had it not been for his advice, I would fail. (과거형 도치 + 현재 결과절 불일치)
- 정답: Were it not for his advice, I would fail.
**4. 기출 변형 패턴**
- `___ + 명사(구), 주어 + would + 동사원형` 형태로 빈칸에 Were/Had 고르게 하는 유형
- 밑줄 친 부분 어법상 틀린 것 고르기에서 도치 형태와 결과절 시제 불일치를 심는 유형
- Were it not for / Had it not been for를 If절로 바꿔 쓰는 서술형 변형 유형
**5. 실전 풀이 순서**<br>① 결과절의 동사 형태(원형인지 have p.p.인지)부터 확인 → ② 그에 맞는 도치 형태(Were/Had)를 역산 → ③ 도치 주어 it과 for + 명사구 구조가 맞는지 재확인

## ✏️ Practice
**A. 어법상 틀린 것 고르기**<br>① Were it not for the traffic, we would have arrived on time.<br>② Had it not been for her encouragement, he wouldn't have tried again.<br>③ Were it not for your mistake, this problem wouldn't happen.<br>④ Had it not been for the sudden rain, the picnic would be cancelled.
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. Were it not for the accident, the road would have been clear by now.
2. Had it not been for the coach, she doesn't win the championship.
3. Were it not for his stubbornness, they would have solved it already.
**C. Make your own sentence**
- "만약 그의 조언이 없었다면" (과거 사실 반대)을 Had it not been for로 시작하는 문장으로 만들어보세요.
- "만약 네 실수가 없다면" (현재 사실 반대)을 Were it not for로 시작하는 문장으로 만들어보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.** 정답 ④ — 결과절이 would be(원형)인데 Had it not been for(과거 도치)를 써서 시제 불일치. Were it not for로 고쳐야 함.
**B.**
1. → Were it not for the accident, the road would be clear by now. (결과절이 현재형이므로 도치도 현재형으로)
2. → Had it not been for the coach, she wouldn't have won the championship. (과거 사실 반대이므로 조동사도 have p.p.로)
3. → Were it not for his stubbornness, they would solve it already. / 또는 Had it not been for ~, would have solved 형태로 시제 통일
**C. (예시 답안)**
- Had it not been for his advice, I would have made a big mistake.
- Were it not for your mistake, this problem wouldn't happen.
