## 📖 Confusion Note

**1. 각 단어 기본 뜻**

- adopt: (정책·방법·제안·태도 등을) 그대로 받아들여 채택하다 / (아이를) 입양하다
- adapt: 새로운 상황·환경·목적에 맞게 스스로 또는 대상을 바꾸다, 적응시키다

**2. 구별 포인트 (판단 기준 하나로 요약)**
→ **"그 대상이 바뀌는가, 안 바뀌는가?"** adopt는 원래 형태 그대로 수용, adapt는 형태·방식을 변형해서 맞춤. 목적어가 policy/plan/method처럼 '이미 존재하는 안'이면 둘 다 올 수 있지만, 뒤에 to가 붙거나 '맞춰서'라는 의미가 있으면 adapt.

**3. 대표 예문 비교**

- The board voted to **adopt** the budget as proposed. (제안된 그대로 채택)
- The novel was **adapted** for the screen. (형식을 바꿔 각색)
- Many companies **adopted** remote work during the pandemic. (제도를 도입)
- Employees had to **adapt** their routines to remote work. (자신의 방식을 조정)

**4. 자주 하는 실수 (X → O)**

- X: The story was adopted into a movie. → O: The story was **adapted** into a movie. (각색은 형식 변경이므로 adapt)
- X: We need to adapt this rule for next year (그대로 도입하려는 의도인데 adapt를 씀) → O: We need to **adopt** this rule for next year.
- X: She adapted a stray cat. → O: She **adopted** a stray cat. (입양은 항상 adopt)

**5. 시험 포인트**

- "adapted from/for" 형태로 각색·개작을 나타내는 표현은 수능·모의고사 지문 출처 표기에 자주 등장 (예: "Adapted from ○○○").
- 목적어가 사람(아이)이면 무조건 adopt.
- "adapt A to B / adapt to B" 전치사 to와 함께 쓰이는지 확인하면 구별에 도움.

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct word**

1. The city council will (adopt / adapt) the new traffic law starting next month.
2. This screenplay was (adopted / adapted) from a bestselling novel.
3. Animals living in the desert have (adopted / adapted) to extreme heat.

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. The couple adapted a baby girl last year.
2. We should adapt this successful model exactly as it is, without any change.
3. The play was adopted for a younger audience, with simpler dialogue.

**C. Make your own sentence (두 단어 각각 활용)**

- adopt를 사용한 문장 1개
- adapt를 사용한 문장 1개

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. adopt (법을 그대로 도입)
2. adapted (소설을 각색)
3. adapted (환경에 적응)

**B.**

1. The couple **adopted** a baby girl last year. (입양은 항상 adopt)
2. We should **adopt** this successful model exactly as it is, without any change. (변경 없이 그대로 받아들임)
3. The play was **adapted** for a younger audience, with simpler dialogue. (형식·내용을 바꿔 각색)

**C.** (예시 — 자유 작문이므로 학생 답안에 따라 채점)

- adopt: Our school decided to adopt a new grading policy.
- adapt: I had to adapt my study plan after moving to a new school.
