## 📖 Confusion Note

1. **각 단어 기본 뜻**
    - compliment: (명) 칭찬 / (동) 칭찬하다 — 대상은 주로 **사람**
    - complement: (명) 보완물, (문법) 보어 / (동) 보완하다, 완전하게 하다 — 대상은 주로 **사물·성질**
2. **구별 포인트**
    - 딱 하나만 기억: "사람을 향한 좋은 말"이면 compliment, "무언가를 채워서 완전하게 만든다"면 complement
    - 스펠링 팁: complEment의 E = Extra(더해서 완성시킴)
3. **대표 예문 비교**
    - Compliment: "You look great today!" she said as a **compliment**.
    - Complement: Good lighting can **complement** a room's design.
4. **자주 하는 실수 (X→O)**
    - X: Thank you for the complement. → O: Thank you for the **compliment**.
    - X: The subject compliment comes after a linking verb. → O: The subject **complement** comes after a linking verb.
    - X: This wine complements the cheese well. (철자를 compliments로 착각) → O: complements가 맞는 표현이지만 뜻은 "어울리다"임에 유의
5. **시험 포인트**
    - 내신 서술형에서 "칭찬하다"를 영작할 때 complement로 잘못 쓰는 오류 최다 발생
    - 문법 단원(문장의 형식)에서 "주격보어/목적격보어"는 반드시 complement로 표기

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the correct word**

1. My teacher gave me a nice ___ (compliment / complement) on my essay.
2. A good soundtrack can really ___ (compliment / complement) a movie.
3. In "They elected her president," "president" is an object ___ (compliment / complement).

**B. Fix the mistake**

1. He paid her a nice complement about her cooking.
2. The two paintings compliment each other beautifully in the gallery.
3. I always complement my friends when they do well.

**C. Make your own sentence** (두 단어 각각 활용)

- compliment를 활용한 문장 1개
- complement를 활용한 문장 1개

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. compliment
2. complement
3. complement

**B.**

1. He paid her a nice **compliment** about her cooking. (칭찬이므로 compliment)
2. The two paintings **complement** each other beautifully in the gallery. (서로 어울린다는 뜻이므로 complement)
3. I always **compliment** my friends when they do well. (사람을 칭찬하는 것이므로 compliment)

**C.** (예시 답안)

- She complimented me on my new haircut.
- The scarf complements the dress perfectly.
