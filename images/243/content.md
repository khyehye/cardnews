## 📖 Writing Pattern Note
**1. 전환 공식**
- 단순부정사: 주어 + 동사 + to V → 본동사와 같은 시점 또는 그 이후
- 완료부정사: 주어 + 동사 + to have p.p. → 본동사보다 이전 시점
- that절 문장 ↔ 부정사 문장 전환 시, that절의 시제가 본동사보다 앞서 있으면 완료부정사를 쓴다.
**2. 단계별 전환 순서**<br>① that절의 주어와 본문장 주어가 같은지 확인한다.<br>② that절의 시제가 본동사 시제보다 앞서는지(과거/과거완료) 판단한다.<br>③ 앞선다면 to have p.p., 같거나 이후라면 to V로 바꾼다.
**3. 자주 틀리는 포인트**
- already, last year, before 같은 과거 신호어를 무시하고 단순부정사를 쓰는 실수
- 완료부정사의 부정형 어순 오류: to not have p.p. (X) → not to have p.p. (O)
- 수동태 완료부정사 어순 실수: to have been p.p. 형태를 놓치는 경우 (예: is said to have been built)
**4. 대표 예문 (기본형 → 전환형)**
- It seems that she was tired. → She seems to have been tired.
- It is believed that he lied. → He is believed to have lied.
- I am sorry that I didn't call you. → I am sorry not to have called you.
**5. 서술형 채점 포인트 (감점 요인)**
- 시제 판단 없이 무조건 to V만 쓰는 경우 감점
- 완료부정사의 not 위치 오류(not to have p.p.가 아닌 to not have p.p.) 감점
- 수동태 결합 시 to have been p.p.를 to be p.p.로 잘못 쓰는 경우 감점

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Transform the sentence**
1. It seems that they finished the project. → ______
2. It is said that the actress was very kind. → ______
3. I am glad that I didn't lose the game. → ______
**B. Fix the mistake**
1. He seems to lose his wallet yesterday. → ______
2. She is believed to be rich in her twenties (지금은 아님, 과거 얘기). → ______
3. I'm sorry to not have called you sooner. → ______
**C. Write your own**<br>소문(rumor)에 대한 문장을 완료부정사를 사용해서 자유롭게 영작해보세요. (예: 그가 부자였다고 한다.)

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. They seem to have finished the project.
2. The actress is said to have been very kind.
3. I am glad not to have lost the game.
**B.**
1. He seems to have lost his wallet yesterday. (과거에 잃어버린 일 → 완료부정사)
2. She is believed to have been rich in her twenties. (과거 시점 얘기 → to have been)
3. I'm sorry not to have called you sooner. (not의 위치는 to have 앞)
**C.** (예시 답안)<br>He is said to have been rich before the war.
