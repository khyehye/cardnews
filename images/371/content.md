## 📖 Expression Note
**1. 쓰는 상황**<br>사과, 변명, 자기 행동을 설명할 때. "일부러 그런 게 아니라 나도 모르게 그렇게 됐다"는 뉘앙스를 전달하고 싶을 때 사용한다.
**2. 격식도**<br>캐주얼~중립. 친구나 동료 사이 대화에서 자연스럽게 쓰이며, 격식 있는 자리에서는 "It was involuntary" 같은 표현으로 대체 가능하다.
**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**
- A: Why did you eat the whole cake? / B: I couldn't help it, it looked so good.
- A: You keep checking your phone. / B: I know, I can't help it, I'm waiting for a call.
**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**<br>can't resist(유혹을 못 이김), couldn't stop myself(행동을 멈추지 못함), have no choice but to(선택지가 없어서 억지로) — 셋 다 뉘앙스가 조금씩 다르다.
**5. 원어민 variation**
- I just couldn't help it.
- I can't help but + 동사원형 (e.g. I can't help but smile.)
- It's out of my control.

## ✏️ Practice
**A. Choose the natural expression**
1. When she saw the surprise party, she ___ (couldn't help crying / can't resist crying).
2. He ___ (can't help but / can't help) laugh whenever he sees that video.
3. The delay was nobody's fault — it ___ (couldn't help it / couldn't be helped).
**B. Complete the dialogue**
1. A: Did you really eat all the cookies? / B: Sorry, I _______ (참을 수 없었어).
2. A: Why do you always tease your brother? / B: I don't know, I just _______ (그러지 않을 수가 없어).
**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**<br>아래 상황 중 하나를 골라 "I couldn't help it" 또는 "I can't help -ing"를 활용한 짧은 대화를 만들어보세요.
- 실수로 남의 비밀을 말해버린 상황
- 다이어트 중인데 야식을 먹어버린 상황

## ✅ Answer Key
**A.**
1. couldn't help crying
2. can't help but
3. couldn't be helped
**B.**
1. couldn't help it
2. can't help it
**C.** (예시 답안)
- I'm sorry, I couldn't help it — she told me not to tell, but I just blurted it out.
- I know I shouldn't have, but I can't help snacking at midnight.
