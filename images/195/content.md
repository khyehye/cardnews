## 📖 Expression Note

**1. 쓰는 상황**
제안, 계약, 프로젝트 등에서 자신이 할 일을 마치고 다음 결정/행동을 상대방에게 넘길 때 사용한다. "이제 당신이 결정할 차례다"라는 뉘앙스.

**2. 격식도**
격식체(Formal)~중격식체. 비즈니스 이메일, 협상, 공식 회의에서 자연스럽다. 친한 친구 사이 캐주얼 대화에서는 다소 딱딱하게 들릴 수 있다.

**3. 대표 예문 & 대화**

- A: "We've submitted our final proposal." B: "Understood — the ball is in your court now."
- "I've completed my portion of the contract. The ball is in your court to review the terms."

**4. 비슷한 다른 표현**

- It's up to you now.
- The decision is in your hands.
- It's over to you.

**5. 원어민 variation**

- "The ball's in your court" (구어체 축약형, court 뒤 소유격 생략 아님 — is의 축약)
- "Now the ball is in their court" (제3자를 가리킬 때)

## ✏️ Practice

**A. Choose the natural expression**

1. After finishing her part of the deal, she said, "I've done everything I can. ___."
(a) The ball is in your court (b) You're pulling my leg (c) It's raining cats and dogs
2. Which sentence uses the idiom correctly in a business context?
(a) The ball is in your court, so let's grab lunch.
(b) We've reviewed your draft — the ball is in your court for final approval.
(c) The ball is in your court means you scored a goal.

**B. Complete the dialogue**
Manager: "I've sent you the budget report and my recommendations."
Employee: "Thanks. So now ___ (공을 넘긴다는 의미로)."

**C. Make your own sentence/dialogue**
회사에서 프로젝트를 마치고 상사에게 결과물을 넘기는 상황을 가정해, "the ball is in your court"를 활용한 짧은 이메일 문장을 작성해보세요.

## ✅ Answer Key

**A.**

1. (a) The ball is in your court
2. (b) — 비즈니스 승인 요청 맥락에서 자연스럽게 쓰였다.

**B.**
"the ball is in your court" (예: "So now the ball is in your court — let me know if you need anything else.")

**C.** (예시 답안)
"I've finished the first draft of the proposal and attached it here. The ball is in your court — please review and let me know your feedback by Friday."
